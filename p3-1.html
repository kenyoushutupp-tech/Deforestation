Introduction

Deforestation is one of the most serious environmental problems in the world today. Every day, large areas of forest are cleared to make space for farming, buildings, and commercial activities. This destruction removes not only trees but also entire ecosystems that support life. Forests serve as the lungs of the Earth, absorbing carbon dioxide and releasing oxygen. They also provide homes for millions of species. When forests disappear, we lose balance in nature, reduce biodiversity, and weaken our planet’s ability to fight climate change.

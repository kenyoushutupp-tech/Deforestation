How can I
help?

Everyone can take part in protecting forests. Join local or online tree-planting programs. Practice the 3Rs: Reduce, Reuse, Recycle to cut down on waste. Avoid buying products that come from unsustainable sources. Share information about forest conservation with your family, friends, or community. Support organizations that work to protect forests and wildlife. Even small steps, when done by many, can create a big impact for a greener and healthier planet.

2025

Statistics

  • Around 10 million hectares of forest are lost each year, which is equal to 27 soccer fields every minute.
  • About 15 to 20 percent of greenhouse gas emissions come from deforestation and forest degradation.
  • Over 80 percent of all land-based animals and plants live in forests, and many of them are now at risk of extinction.
  • If current rates continue, the world's rainforests could disappear within the next 100 years, changing the Earth's climate forever.
